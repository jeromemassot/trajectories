3. Confounder Scaling & Adversarial Tests

Scale confounding pairs as user-configured percentages of the target population.

💡 Why include recycled phone confounders?

In telecommunications, phone numbers are relinquished and reallocated to new individuals after carrier quarantine. Marcus Vance (Boston, 2016–2018) and Clara Oswald (Denver, 2021–2024) both hold +1-555-0199 across different years.

Including this adversarial test case validates that the resolution engine avoids false merges between unrelated people sharing a recycled number, stress-testing the temporal carrier decay (τphone = 730 days) and biological DOB conflict guardrails.
